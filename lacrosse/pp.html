Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application "Lacrosse App : AI Coach" (“we”, “our”, or “us”). It explains how we handle data when you use the app.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. The only data collected is anonymous analytics to help improve the app.

2. Local Data Storage

All app data is stored locally in the app. We do not store personal information on external servers.

3. Analytics

Analytics data is anonymous and used only to improve app performance and user experience. We do not collect names, email addresses, or other personal identifiers.

4. AI Processing

Any information you provide for AI coaching is used only for processing your request and generating the coaching response. It is not stored on our servers after processing, and it is not used for any other purpose.

Sharing data with the AI is discretionary and optional. You may choose not to provide information if you prefer not to use the AI features.

5. AI Analysis and Data Processing

To provide you with personalized lacrosse coaching, biomechanical feedback, and custom training programs, the Lacrosse App utilizes advanced artificial intelligence services provided by Google Gemini (Vertex AI).

1. Data Collection and Sharing

When you use the AI Coach Chat or Video Feedback features, the App collects and securely transmits the following data to Google Gemini for processing:

  • Video Content: Video clips you upload for form analysis.
  • Profile Information: Your position, training goals, age, and skill level.
  • Chat Messages: Any questions or prompts you send to the AI Coach.

2. Use of Data

This data is used solely to generate coaching feedback, analyze your athletic mechanics (e.g., shooting form, stick work), and personalize your training modules. We do not use this data for advertising or marketing.

3. Face Data and Biometrics

The Lacrosse App does not collect, store, or process biometric face data or perform facial recognition. While your face may be visible in training videos, the analysis is strictly limited to biomechanical body movements and athletic form. We do not use any facial identification technology.

4. Third-Party Protection

Data shared with Google Gemini is handled in accordance with industry-standard security protocols. Google provides equal protection for your data as outlined in this policy and does not retain your training videos or personal data for their own model training purposes.

5. Retention

AI analysis results are stored locally on your device. Video content is processed in real-time or near real-time and is not stored permanently by the AI service.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy in the future. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: splopshq@proton.me
