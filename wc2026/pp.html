Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application "World Cup 2026: FWC Predictor" (“we”, “our”, or “us”). It explains how we handle data and privacy for this app.

1. What Data is Collected (and what is NOT)

• Personally Identifiable Information (PII): Zero collected. There are no sign-ups, no accounts, no names, no emails, and no hardware/device identifier tracking.

• Anonymous User ID: The app generates a completely random identifier on the device (for example, anon_1717...) and stores it in local storage/SharedPreferences to associate your presence in chats and locally lock poll predictions.

• Poll Predictions: Individual vote choices are only saved locally on the device so you cannot vote twice. On the server, predictions are stored only as anonymous aggregate count increments (for example, “+1 for Brazil”).

2. Chat Messages & Presence

• Messages: The text you write and your anonymous ID are sent to the Firebase Realtime Database so other users in the room can see them.

• Presence: When you enter a room, a temporary presence flag is set under your anonymous ID. When you leave the room or close the app, it is automatically removed via .onDisconnect().remove().

• Retention: Because no accounts are tied to these messages, they remain transient and anonymous. This makes it possible to clear or purge chat history nodes periodically on the backend database.

3. Security

We use secure connections for any server communication. The app does not retain personal profile data or device identifiers beyond the anonymous ID stored locally for app functionality.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: splopshq@proton.me
