Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 11, 2026

Pregnancy Test Scanner AI Fast ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Images: When you use the scanning feature, the app accesses your device's camera or photo library. These images are processed locally or via secure AI services solely for the purpose of providing test analysis.

Local Data: All scan history and results are stored locally on your device. We do not maintain a central database of your personal scan history.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Provide AI analysis of your pregnancy test.
  • Save your test history on your device for your own review.
  • Improve our AI models (anonymized data only, where applicable).

3. Data Storage and Security

We prioritize your privacy. Your scan history is stored on your device. We do not sell or share your personal health data with third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • RevenueCat: For subscription management.
  • Firebase/Gemini AI: For image analysis.

5. Your Choices

You can delete your scan history at any time within the app's settings. You can also revoke camera and storage permissions through your device settings.
