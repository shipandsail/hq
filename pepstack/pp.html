Privacy Policy

App: Peptide Calculator: PepStack
Effective date: June 27, 2026
Last updated: June 27, 2026

Short version: PepStack stores all your health and tracking data locally on your device. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal health information. The only data that leaves your device is what is necessary to process your subscription through Apple's in-app purchase system.

1. Who We Are

Peptide Calculator: PepStack ("PepStack," "we," "us," or "our") is an application designed to help individuals track peptide doses, manage injection site rotation, calculate reconstitution volumes, and monitor lifestyle metrics. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at:

splopshq@proton.me

2. Information We Collect

2a. Information stored locally on your device

PepStack stores the following data exclusively on your device using Apple's standard local storage (NSUserDefaults / SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us:

  • Peptide compound names, concentrations, and vial sizes you enter
  • Dose log entries (date, time, dose amount, draw volume, injection site)
  • Injection site rotation history
  • Body weight measurements and progress entries
  • Lifestyle tracking data (daily calorie, protein, and water intake)
  • App preferences and settings (theme, onboarding state)
  • Reconstitution calculator inputs

2b. Subscription and purchase data

PepStack uses RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions. When you initiate a purchase, RevenueCat and Apple process the transaction. RevenueCat may collect:

  • A randomly generated anonymous app user ID
  • Purchase receipt data from Apple
  • Device type and operating system version (for debugging purposes)

RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address, or any health data. You can review RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Apple processes all payments. Apple's privacy policy governs how Apple handles your payment information and Apple ID: apple.com/legal/privacy.

2c. Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect, and PepStack does not have the technical capability to access:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Location data
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Contacts or calendar data
  • Crash reports or analytics telemetry
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • Any data from Apple Health / HealthKit

3. How We Use Information

We use information solely to provide and improve the app's functionality:

  • Local health data — used only to display your tracking history, charts, and calculations within the app on your device.
  • Purchase data — used to verify your subscription status and unlock Pro features.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the app experience.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except as described below:

  • RevenueCat — receives anonymous purchase data to manage subscriptions (see Section 2b).
  • Apple Inc. — processes in-app purchases and may retain transaction records in accordance with their privacy policy.
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority. Given that we do not collect personal health data, there is nothing to disclose.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

All health and tracking data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all app data at any time by deleting the PepStack app from your device. Deleting the app permanently removes all locally stored data.

To request deletion of any subscription-related data held by RevenueCat, contact us at splopshq@proton.me and we will submit a deletion request on your behalf.

6. Children's Privacy

PepStack is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

7. Health Data Disclaimer

PepStack is a personal tracking and calculation tool. It is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before beginning any peptide or medication protocol.

8. Security

Because all health data is stored locally on your device, it is protected by your device's built-in security features, including device passcode, Face ID, and Touch ID. We encourage you to keep your device secured. We do not have access to your data and therefore cannot be responsible for breaches originating from unauthorized access to your device.

9. Apple App Store Requirements

PepStack complies with Apple's App Store Review Guidelines and Apple's requirements for apps that handle sensitive data. In accordance with App Store policy:

  • All health-related data entered by users is stored locally and not transmitted to any server.
  • No data collected by the app is used for tracking users across other apps or websites.
  • The app does not share data with data brokers.
  • The app does not use data for advertising purposes.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of any personal data we hold about you (limited to subscription identifiers via RevenueCat).
  • Deletion — request deletion of your data. For local data, delete the app. For subscription data, contact us.
  • Opt-out — there is no behavioral tracking or advertising to opt out of.

If you are a resident of California, the CCPA grants you additional rights regarding personal information. If you are in the EEA or UK, the GDPR applies. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at splopshq@proton.me.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If changes are material, we will notify you via an in-app notice. Continued use of PepStack after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
