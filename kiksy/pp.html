Privacy Policy for Kiksy

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

Kiksy ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Kiksy mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

We do not require you to create an account to use the App. Any personal information you provide (such as your name or due date) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

b. Audio Recordings

Any audio recordings you make using the App are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to your recordings, and we do not upload them to any remote servers.

c. Automated Data Collection

We may use third-party services that collect information used to identify you:

  • Firebase (Google): We use Firebase for basic analytics and crash reporting to improve the App's performance.
  • RevenueCat: We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions and purchases. RevenueCat may collect transaction data and anonymous identifiers to verify your subscription status.

2. Use of Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality.
  • Monitor and analyze App usage and trends.
  • Process and manage in-app purchases.
  • Improve the App's user experience.

3. Data Storage and Security

Because your recordings and personal details (name, due date) are stored locally, the security of that data depends on the security of your device. We recommend using device-level security features like passcodes or biometric locks.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Medical Disclaimer

Kiksy is intended for entertainment, bonding, and keepsake purposes only. It is not a medical device. We do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or monitoring. Always consult a healthcare professional for any medical concerns during pregnancy.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
